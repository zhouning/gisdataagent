    2026 Fare Changes

    WTA fares are increasing on September 1, 2026

    You are here:

    1. Home
    2. 2026 Fare Changes

    Who rides for free?

    • Youth 18 and under
    • Personal Care Assistants (accompanying riders with disabilities)

    Who qualifies for Fixed Route reduced fares?

    • Riders 65+
    • All paratransit eligible riders
    • Veterans
    • Those who meet disability or income-based eligibility
    • Students

    Fare Capping: Pay as You Ride

    No need to buy a pass in advance! When you use the same payment method (Umo Card, App, or Bank Card), your fares are automatically capped:

    • Fixed Route Fare Caps: Pay for three rides in one day ($6) or thirty rides in one month ($60) and the rest of your rides are free.
      • $30 per month Fare Cap for Reduced Fare riders.
    • Paratransit Fare Caps: Pay for two rides in one day ($6) or ten rides in one month ($30) and the rest of your rides are free.
    • Note: Cash payments do not count toward your fare cap.

    Gold Cards are Becoming Reduced Fare Cards

    Starting September 1, Umo Gold Cards will become Reduced Fare Cards.

    Do you need a new card?

    • If you have a Umo Gold Card: No.
    • If you have an older version: Yes, please visit us to update your card by September 1.

    Why is WTA increasing fares?

    • As is the case for most transit agencies, the cost to provide service (wages, benefits, vehicles, insurance, fuel, utilities, etc.) has been steadily rising.
    • WTA’s primary source of revenue, local sales tax, is not keeping pace.
    • The 2026 fare increase is necessary to prevent WTA from having to reduce bus service in the future.

    When was the last time WTA increased fares?

    • WTA last increased its cash fare in 2008.

    How do WTA’s fares compare?

    Most transit agencies in Washington and its surrounding regions charge between $2 and $3 for a standard adult fare. WTA’s proposed $2 fare would keep us in line with neighboring systems.

    In Washington State, typical monthly Fixed Route and Paratransit fare caps are $60 to $90.
