NARRATIVE AND PLANS ATTACHMENT
TYPE 1 REZONING FROM B3-2 TO B2-3
Project Description
The property is improved with a three story mixed use building with two
parking spaces. The applicant seeks to rezone to allow a residential dwelling
unit on the ground floor converting the building from 2 dwelling units to three
dwelling units. No commercial use is proposed and building height will
remain at 34.6 feet.
Lot Area: 2088 sq ft
MLA: 696 sq ft
FAR: 1.75
Parking: 2 off street parking spaces
Setbacks:
• 32 feet from building to alley
• 3 feet from garage to alley
• 11.5 feet from curb to front of building
Building Height: 34'6"
