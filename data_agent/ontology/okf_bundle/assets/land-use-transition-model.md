# Meaning

The OWL ontology defines typed relations between a land parcel, its source and
target land-use states, the transition process, applicable spatial constraints,
supporting policy, and approval evidence.

# Decision Boundary

The model supports semantic interpretation and auxiliary precheck. It does not
replace statutory approval or an administrative decision.

The approved replay contract is [Heping land-conversion precheck](/computations/heping-land-conversion-precheck.md).
