# Natural Resource Ontology Knowledge Bundle

This bundle describes the governed natural-resource ontology, its semantic
mapping assets, and sanctioned customer-demo computations. OWL/RDF remains the
authoritative formal model; this bundle is its human- and agent-readable
knowledge, provenance, and computation-contract companion.

# Knowledge Assets

* [Natural-resource ontology](/assets/natural-resource-ontology.md) - governed OWL/RDF domain model and published package.
* [Land-use transition model](/assets/land-use-transition-model.md) - states, processes, evidence requirements, and decision boundary.
* [Semantic mapping catalog](/assets/semantic-mapping-catalog.md) - mappings from source fields and values to ontology concepts.

# Attested Computations
